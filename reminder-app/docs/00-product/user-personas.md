# User Personas

## Persona Framework
Each persona includes goals, pain points, behavior patterns, trust concerns, and linked stories to support end-to-end traceability.

## P-01 Busy Professional
- **Profile:** Knowledge worker with high meeting density and frequent service appointments.
- **Primary goals:** Avoid missed meetings/appointments; reduce planning overhead.
- **Pain points:** Important emails buried in threads; little time for manual calendar management.
- **Behavior traits:** Heavy mobile messaging usage; inbox triage in short bursts.
- **Trust needs:** Clear permission boundaries and confidence reminders are accurate.
- **Linked stories:** US-001, US-003, US-006, US-008.

## P-02 Parent Managing Family Schedules
- **Profile:** Coordinates school, health, and activity commitments across household calendars.
- **Primary goals:** Ensure family events are not missed; reduce cognitive load.
- **Pain points:** High volume of school communications; variable message formats.
- **Behavior traits:** Relies on messaging apps for real-time coordination.
- **Trust needs:** Reliable reminders and transparent event source visibility.
- **Linked stories:** US-004, US-006, US-008, US-010.

## P-03 University Student
- **Profile:** Manages classes, exams, administration deadlines, and extracurricular activities.
- **Primary goals:** Stay organized without maintaining multiple manual reminder systems.
- **Pain points:** Event details spread across notices and enrollment emails.
- **Behavior traits:** Mobile-first; high responsiveness to short reminder prompts.
- **Trust needs:** Low-friction onboarding and predictable reminder timing.
- **Linked stories:** US-005, US-007, US-009.

## P-04 Freelancer / Consultant
- **Profile:** Handles client calls, interviews, project checkpoints, and ad-hoc bookings.
- **Primary goals:** Maintain punctuality and professional reliability.
- **Pain points:** Event emails from many senders; repetitive manual reminder setup.
- **Behavior traits:** Uses messaging channels more than inbox for immediate awareness.
- **Trust needs:** Duplicate prevention and dependable channel delivery.
- **Linked stories:** US-003, US-008, US-010, US-011.

## P-05 Small Business Owner
- **Profile:** Balances operations, customer appointments, vendor calls, and admin tasks.
- **Primary goals:** Protect schedule integrity while multitasking across business priorities.
- **Pain points:** High operational inbox noise; missed commitments impact service quality.
- **Behavior traits:** Needs concise, reliable reminders and simple controls.
- **Trust needs:** Operational reliability, clear settings, and audit-friendly history.
- **Linked stories:** US-006, US-008, US-011, US-013.

## Accessibility and Inclusion Considerations
- Reminder and settings language should be concise and plain-language oriented.
- Time and date handling must be clear across locales/time zones.
- Notification controls should support users with varied digital literacy.
- Channel preference flexibility should account for access and regional constraints.
