Executive View

Business + Delivery Alignment

The product solves a high-frequency behavior gap: users receive event details in email but fail to consistently convert them into reminders. V1 focuses on a reliable core journey—connect Gmail, detect event emails, extract details, schedule reminders, and deliver through WhatsApp (plus optional SMS).

The product baseline is now consolidated into a single requirements authority, including business requirements, functional and non-functional requirements, risk controls, compliance expectations, and operational reporting standards.

Vision & Strategic Outcomes

  • Vision: become the trusted layer between inbox activity and real-world follow-through.
  • Strategic outcomes: reduce missed commitments, lower manual effort, increase channel effectiveness, and strengthen trust through transparent consent and security posture.
  • Execution links: vision outcomes map into goals and measurable success criteria in requirements.md.

Requirements Baseline

Requirements now include: document purpose, problem statement, business context, goals, success criteria, business requirements (BR), functional requirements (FR), non-functional requirements (NFR), assumptions, constraints, dependencies, risks, compliance, reporting, and phase-2 considerations.

The former BRD file has been consolidated into requirements.md to remove duplication and maintain one authoritative baseline.

User Segments & Personas

Five target personas are defined: busy professionals, parents, students, freelancers/consultants, and small business owners. Each persona includes goals, pains, behavior traits, trust requirements, and linked stories for prioritization traceability.

Story Backlog

The V1 backlog includes 13 stories with explicit priority levels (P0/P1/P2), covering onboarding, detection/extraction, reminder workflow, preferences, optional sync, and operational visibility.

Acceptance & Release Gates

Twelve acceptance criteria (AC-001..AC-012) provide Given/When/Then testability and map each story to corresponding requirements. P0 acceptance pass is required for V1 release sign-off.

V1 Scope Boundaries

  • Must ship: P0 stories, core detect-extract-schedule-notify flow, duplicate prevention, and operational logging.
  • Conditional: optional Google Calendar sync may defer without blocking V1.
  • Out-of-scope: non-Gmail providers, enterprise team workflows, advanced AI conversation/rules, and voice channels.
