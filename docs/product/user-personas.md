# User Personas

## 1) Busy Professional
**Background:** Works in a fast-paced environment with multiple meetings, appointments, and personal commitments each week.

**Goals:** Stay on schedule, avoid missed meetings, and reduce manual task overhead.

**Pain Points:** Important confirmation emails get buried; reminder setup is inconsistent during busy periods.

**How the product helps them:** Automatically captures appointment details from email and delivers timely reminders through channels they notice.

## 2) Parent Managing School Activities
**Background:** Balances household logistics, school communications, extracurricular events, and family appointments.

**Goals:** Keep track of school meetings, activity schedules, and family commitments without missing key times.

**Pain Points:** School emails are frequent and time-sensitive; details are easy to miss in long threads.

**How the product helps them:** Detects event-related school emails, turns them into reminders, and reduces reliance on memory.

## 3) University Student
**Background:** Manages classes, exams, office hours, campus activities, and administrative deadlines.

**Goals:** Stay organized and avoid missing academic events and obligations.

**Pain Points:** Email volume is high; event details are spread across multiple systems and notices.

**How the product helps them:** Pulls key event details from confirmation and notice emails, then reminds them before each commitment.

## 4) Freelancer / Consultant
**Background:** Handles multiple clients, discovery calls, project check-ins, and interviews across varied schedules.

**Goals:** Maintain reliability with clients and avoid no-shows that affect reputation and revenue.

**Pain Points:** Client scheduling emails arrive from many sources; manual reminder setup is repetitive.

**How the product helps them:** Converts client-related emails into reminders automatically, improving punctuality and professional consistency.

## 5) Small Business Owner
**Background:** Oversees vendor calls, customer appointments, service bookings, and operational tasks.

**Goals:** Keep commitments on time while managing many business responsibilities.

**Pain Points:** Operational inbox traffic is heavy; important event messages are easy to overlook.

**How the product helps them:** Surfaces event commitments from email and delivers reminders that protect service quality and follow-through.
