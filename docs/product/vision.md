# Product Vision

The Email-to-Reminder Assistant is designed to become the default personal layer between inbox activity and real-world commitments. Over time, the product should make event follow-through effortless: when an important email arrives, users should not need to manually copy details, create calendar entries, or remember to set reminders.

The product addresses a persistent problem space: missed events caused by fragmented workflows, inbox overload, and unreliable reminder habits.

## Mission

Help people consistently act on important email-based commitments by automatically converting event emails into timely, trusted reminders.

## North Star Outcome

Users can rely on the assistant to reduce missed appointments and deadlines, with minimal manual setup, so they feel more organized and less stressed in daily life.

## Future Product Direction

### Email-to-action automation
Expand from event reminders to broader email-driven actions, such as confirming attendance, surfacing required follow-ups, and tracking important commitments.

### Intelligent reminders
Evolve reminder quality with context-aware timing, priority handling, and behavior-informed nudges that improve user follow-through.

### Personal assistant capabilities
Grow into a proactive personal assistant experience that helps users manage commitments, preparation steps, and schedule confidence.

### Integration with messaging platforms
Broaden reminder delivery and interaction options across major messaging channels so users receive alerts where they are most responsive.
