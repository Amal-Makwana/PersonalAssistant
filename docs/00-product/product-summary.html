Product Positioning

The product closes the workflow gap between receiving an event email and actually being reminded in time. It transforms inbox signals into structured events and policy-driven reminders, beginning with Gmail + WhatsApp and extending via optional SMS and Calendar sync.

Mission: Help users consistently act on email-based commitments by automating event capture and reminder delivery.

Business Context

Business Driver

Missed commitments create stress, churn risk, and trust erosion.

Market Need

Users receive event information via email but fail to convert it into reliable reminders.

Strategic Intent

Build a dependable “email received → reminder guaranteed” experience.

Goals and Success Metrics

  • Reduce missed events among active users.
  • Increase onboarding completion for Gmail connection.
  • Maintain high extraction, scheduling, and delivery reliability.
Metric GroupExamples
BusinessWAU, retention, reduction in self-reported missed events
AdoptionGmail connect completion, WhatsApp opt-in, SMS enablement
OperationalExtraction success, scheduling success, delivery success by channel
TechnicalIngestion-to-event latency, P95 dispatch latency, duplicate prevention accuracy

Target Users

Primary

Busy professionals, parent coordinators, freelancers/consultants.

Secondary

University students and small business operators.

Core User Need

Reliable reminders without manual event transcription from email.

Functional Requirements (PRD FR-01..FR-11)

Core capabilities include Google sign-in, Gmail authorization, event detection and extraction, event persistence, policy-based reminder scheduling, WhatsApp delivery, optional SMS, optional Calendar sync, duplicate prevention, and preference management.

Non-Functional and Operational Expectations

  • Secure OAuth-based access and privacy-safe data handling.
  • Scalable processing for growing mailbox and reminder volumes.
  • Observability via monitoring dashboards and structured logs.
  • Support readiness with failure triage and incident ownership.

V1 Scope Boundaries

In Scope: Gmail-based automation loop, fixed reminder timings, WhatsApp delivery, optional SMS/Calendar, duplicate prevention.

Out of Scope: Non-Gmail providers, recurring rule engines, enterprise admin/team workflows, conversational AI features.

Traceability Chain

Vision → Goals → Requirements (FR IDs) → User Stories (US IDs) → Acceptance Criteria (testable outcomes).

This alignment ensures business intent remains testable and measurable throughout delivery.

Key Risks and Dependencies

  • Email parsing ambiguity may affect extraction quality.
  • Messaging provider outages can impact notification SLAs.
  • External API changes (Google or messaging providers) may affect delivery timelines.
