1) Vision, Mission, and Strategic Horizon

Strategic Narrative

The product closes the fragile step between receiving event emails and setting reminders. It turns inbox receipt into reliable follow-through.

Mission

Transform email-based commitments into dependable reminders with minimal user effort and high trust.

Long-Term Vision

Evolve from reminders into broader email-to-action assistance while preserving privacy, control, and reliability.

Strategic Goals:
  • Deliver dependable detect → extract → schedule → notify workflow.
  • Build trust through transparency and preference control.
  • Establish an extensible foundation for future assistant capabilities.
Source: docs/00-product/requirements.md

2) Product Requirements (Enterprise PRD Synthesis)

Problem to Solve

  • Users miss commitments due to manual email parsing and setup friction.
  • Important details are buried in threads, causing inconsistent reminder setup.
  • Passive notification channels reduce action reliability.

Business & Product Outcomes

  • Reduce missed-event incidents.
  • Lower user effort to convert event emails into reminders.
  • Increase confidence through predictable, high-attention delivery.

Success Metrics

  • Business: WAU, retention, missed-event reduction.
  • Adoption: Gmail completion, WhatsApp opt-in, SMS enablement.
  • Operational/Technical: extraction & delivery success, latency, dedupe accuracy.
Functional Requirements Coverage (FR-01..FR-11):

Google sign-in, Gmail authorization, event detection/extraction/persistence, default reminder schedule, WhatsApp delivery, optional SMS, optional Calendar sync, duplicate prevention, and preference management.

Must: FR-01,02,03,04,05,06,07,10 Should: FR-08, FR-09 Could: FR-11

3) User Personas and Behavioral Drivers

Primary Personas

  • Busy Professional: needs high confidence under heavy context switching.
  • Parent Coordinator: manages multi-source family commitments.
  • Freelancer / Consultant: balances client, billing, and schedule signals.

Secondary Personas

  • University Student: event details scattered across institutions.
  • Small Business Operator: high inbox operational load with frequent deadlines.

Shared User Need

“If it arrives in email, I should be reminded in time without manually rebuilding the event.”

5) Acceptance Validation Snapshot

Acceptance criteria define implementation-ready “Given / When / Then” outcomes and maintain one-to-one mapping with US-01 through US-11.

Lifecycle Stage Representative Acceptance Coverage
Onboarding Successful Google sign-in, explicit consent transparency, and preference persistence behavior.
Automation Core Event email classification, extraction of title/date/time/location, persistence, and dedupe enforcement.
Reminder Delivery Default schedule generation and channel-specific dispatch with status/failure logging semantics.
Ecosystem Sync Optional Google Calendar synchronization for enabled users with update consistency expectations.

6) V1 Scope Boundaries and Release Readiness

In Scope (V1)

  • Google auth + Gmail processing
  • Event extraction/persistence
  • 4h/1h/15m reminder policy
  • WhatsApp + optional SMS
  • Optional Calendar sync
  • Duplicate prevention + preferences

Out of Scope (V1)

  • Non-Gmail providers
  • Recurring rule engines
  • Team/admin collaboration workflows
  • Voice/conversational assistant features
  • Advanced automation chains

Readiness Rule

V1 is scope-complete when FR-01 through FR-11 are implemented, story-mapped, and acceptance-validated.

Open Questions / Gaps

The following gaps are explicitly called out across product markdown and should be resolved before full production hardening.

  • Extraction confidence threshold: no numeric policy for auto-schedule vs manual confirmation gate.
  • Reminder SLA targets: channel-level timing commitments are not quantified.
  • Jurisdiction-specific consent artifacts: privacy disclosure scope is identified but not finalized.
  • Fallback policy ambiguity: WhatsApp failure → SMS fallback in V1 is undecided.
  • Operational ownership depth: escalation windows/runbooks are referenced but not detailed in this product set.
  • KPI targets: KPIs are defined, but threshold targets and launch guardrails are not yet quantified.
