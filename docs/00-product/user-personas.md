# User Personas

## Persona 1: Busy Professional (Primary)
**Profile:** Knowledge worker with dense schedules across meetings, healthcare visits, and personal commitments.

**Goals:**
- Avoid missed commitments that impact performance or reputation.
- Reduce manual admin work for event tracking.

**Frustrations:**
- Confirmation emails get buried in high-volume inboxes.
- Manual reminder setup is easy to skip during context switching.

**Behaviors:**
- Checks messaging apps more often than email notifications.
- Relies on quick mobile interactions between tasks.

**Environment:**
- Mobile-first throughout the day; desktop during focused work blocks.
- Frequent time pressure and meeting overload.

## Persona 2: Parent Coordinator (Primary)
**Profile:** Parent managing school communications, family logistics, and household appointments.

**Goals:**
- Keep family schedule synchronized.
- Prevent missed school and healthcare commitments.

**Frustrations:**
- Critical details are hidden in long school email chains.
- High cognitive load from multi-person scheduling.

**Behaviors:**
- Reviews emails in batches, often after receiving many messages.
- Prefers reminders in high-visibility messaging channels.

**Environment:**
- Multi-device usage with frequent interruptions.
- Shared family responsibilities and variable daily routines.

## Persona 3: Freelancer / Consultant (Primary)
**Profile:** Independent professional managing client calls, interviews, and project coordination.

**Goals:**
- Maintain punctuality and reliability as a service differentiator.
- Prevent no-shows that affect revenue and trust.

**Frustrations:**
- Client event details arrive from varied senders and formats.
- Manual event-to-reminder conversion is repetitive and error-prone.

**Behaviors:**
- Operates across multiple concurrent engagements.
- Uses messaging apps as primary real-time communication channel.

**Environment:**
- Dynamic schedule with frequent changes.
- Mobile usage during travel and between appointments.

## Persona 4: University Student (Secondary)
**Profile:** Student balancing classes, exams, office hours, and academic administration.

**Goals:**
- Stay aware of time-sensitive academic commitments.
- Reduce organizational overhead while juggling coursework.

**Frustrations:**
- Event details are spread across fragmented institutional emails.
- Important dates are easy to miss in high email volume periods.

**Behaviors:**
- Batch-processes email and reacts late to notices.
- Responds better to direct reminders than passive inbox items.

**Environment:**
- Device switching between laptop and smartphone.
- Irregular schedules across weekdays and exam cycles.

## Persona 5: Small Business Operator (Secondary)
**Profile:** Owner-operator coordinating vendor appointments, customer bookings, and internal operations.

**Goals:**
- Ensure on-time attendance for service quality.
- Maintain consistency despite high operational load.

**Frustrations:**
- Operational emails compete with transactional noise.
- Missed events create customer dissatisfaction and business risk.

**Behaviors:**
- Handles scheduling reactively while managing daily operations.
- Prefers concise, actionable reminders.

**Environment:**
- Fast-paced multitasking context with limited planning windows.
- Strong reliance on mobile messaging for immediate attention.
